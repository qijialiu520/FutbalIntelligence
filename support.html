Quick answers

Common questions

How do I start training?

Open any game from the home screen, view the guide, and choose Solo Training. Pass To Goal, Check Shoulder, and Through Ball solo training are free. Play & Go and Pinpoint Cross solo training require Premium Lifetime. Check Shoulder includes one extra setup screen for scenario and turn controls.

How does Nearby Match work?

Open the same game on two to eight supported iPhones on the same local network. Choose Nearby Match, host on one phone, join on the others, and accept Apple’s invitation. Nearby Match is available for all five games and requires Premium Lifetime and Local Network permission.

Why can’t another iPhone find the match?

Confirm the iPhones are on the same local network, Bluetooth and Wi-Fi are available, and Futbal Intelligence has Local Network permission in Settings. Leave and reopen the lobby after changing permission.

How do I restore Premium?

Tap the crown on the home screen, then choose Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account that originally purchased or redeemed Premium Lifetime. Premium is a non-consumable purchase, not a subscription.

Where is my training history?

Tap the weekly metrics on the home screen. Results are stored only on your device. Deleting the app removes local history, subject to device backups you control through Apple.

How do I delete my data?

There is no Qijia Liu account or server record. Delete the app to remove locally stored preferences and training history, subject to any device backup you control through Apple.
