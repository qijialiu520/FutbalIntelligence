Privacy Policy

What stays on your device, what is shared during nearby play, and what Futbal Intelligence never collects.

Effective September 20, 2026

01

Data collection

Futbal Intelligence does not send personal data, gameplay history, analytics, advertising identifiers, or tracking data to Qijia Liu or to an analytics service. There is no account registration or login.

02

Information stored on your device

Your player name, preferences, and up to 100 recent training results are stored on your device so the app can remember your setup and show progress. Deleting the app removes this locally stored information, subject to any device backup you control through Apple.

03

Nearby matches

If you choose Nearby Match, the app requests local-network access and exchanges the player name you entered, match settings, round state, and scores directly with nearby participants for that session. Other participants can see that name and those match results. Qijia Liu does not receive or retain this information. Leave the lobby or end the match to stop the exchange.

04

Premium access and payments

Apple processes purchases, restores, and Offer Code redemption through StoreKit. The app reads Apple’s signed entitlement only to determine whether Premium features are unlocked. Qijia Liu does not receive payment-card details. Apple retains purchase records under Apple’s policies.

05

Permissions

Local-network access is requested only after you choose to host or join a Nearby Match. You can change this permission in iOS Settings. Solo training remains available without it. The app does not request location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, health, or tracking permission.

06

Your choices and deletion

There is no Qijia Liu account or server-side personal-data record to delete. You can stop a nearby exchange by leaving the session, change Local Network access in Settings, and remove app-stored preferences and history by deleting the app.

07

Children and regional operation

The app is a general-audience sports training game and is not directed specifically to children. Its privacy behavior is consistent across supported regions. Apple storefront and purchase availability may vary by region.

08

Contact and policy updates

For privacy questions, email kickhard.kicksmart@gmail.com. If this policy changes, the effective date will be updated and the current policy will remain available at this address.
